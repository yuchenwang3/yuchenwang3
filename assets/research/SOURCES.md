# Profile research thumbnails

- `occamy-mark*.svg`: official Occamy logo geometry from `Accio-Lab/occamy`, `website/public/favicon.svg`; colors adapted for light/dark backgrounds. Generated by `scripts/render_profile.py`.
- `cineflow-system.png`: Figure 7 (system overview), page 4 of the [CineFlow report](https://yuchenwang3.github.io/assets/pdf/projects/cineflow-paper.pdf).
- `prefill-timeline.png`: Figure 3 (micro-batching timeline), page 5 of the [Dynamic Prefill report](https://yuchenwang3.github.io/assets/pdf/projects/dynamic-prefill-online-packing-report.pdf).

The report figures are unmodified crops rendered with Poppler. They are method diagrams, not generated-video samples or measured performance plots.

Reproduction (PDFs downloaded as `cineflow-paper.pdf` and `dynamic-prefill-online-packing-report.pdf`):

```sh
pdftoppm -f 4 -l 4 -scale-to 3200 -x 1280 -y 280 -W 980 -H 360 -png -singlefile cineflow-paper.pdf cineflow-system
pdftoppm -f 5 -l 5 -scale-to 3200 -x 345 -y 1910 -W 1790 -H 585 -png -singlefile dynamic-prefill-online-packing-report.pdf prefill-timeline
```

Open-source marks in the README are the organizations' own GitHub avatars, not invented product logos.
